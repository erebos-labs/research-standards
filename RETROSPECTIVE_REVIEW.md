## Evidence and limits

Ordinary proofs receive substantive separate AI mathematical review. Finite results retain their exact enumeration domains, reduction arguments, independent checkers and rejection controls. Numerical and educational demonstrations remain experiments. Running examples never substitute for a general proof.

Named IRDCS, ordered-matrix and generic Snaky Lean statements receive statement-fidelity review, source builds and axiom/trust review. IRDCS 590 project modules and 2781 external dependency modules, and Ordered 40 project modules and 2022 external dependency modules, are built in separate source-build phases. Fresh final statement/axiom checks use the newly built dependency outputs. This is not a single joint cold-build invocation or a compiler bootstrap. Pinned Lean 4.19.0/4.34.1 compiler distributions and their kernels remain trusted; applicable named headline closures use propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound. Ordinary historical family173 releases are not thereby Lean formalizations.

Boolean's newer conditional ordinary arguments remain separate from historical endpoint formal records and unreconstructed global profile/placement bookkeeping. Its n14 source/root is not bundled in those releases. Snaky's concrete generated 20-move certificate remains intentionally omitted, so its public package proves generic conditional transfer rather than an unconditional self-contained 20-move application. PR349 remains a draft; its standalone model-specific obstruction does not claim complete repository integration, a new multiplication bound or a speedup.

The square clearance proof is distinct from sampled triangular comparisons and imposed-contact animation. Desktop browser checks of the educational experiments do not certify listening, export, mobile, accessibility, other browsers or real-world physical/traffic performance. In the traffic model, “exact” refers to the analytic stationary solution; displayed values use JavaScript binary floating point. Explicit wording that makes this distinction is prepared but remains unpublished.

Attribution and reuse terms remain those of each release. Reserved-rights experiments are not open source. Appropriate artifact privacy/metadata checks found no identified unintended disclosure, but cannot guarantee every possible secret or hidden payload is absent. Dated bounded related-work records support attribution and comparison, not first-ever priority. The retrospective search covers known releases and visible announcements; inaccessible/deleted/reply/edit history and unknown prior external contributions remain documented discovery limits.
